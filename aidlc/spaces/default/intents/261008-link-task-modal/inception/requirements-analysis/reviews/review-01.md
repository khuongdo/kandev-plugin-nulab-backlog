### Summary

The intent is well grounded. Q1 (D), Q2 (A) and Q3 (A) trace cleanly to FR1 to FR4, the scope is bounded, the backend contract does not change, and the NFRs are testable. It is READY with two Major gaps, both of which have a simple workaround: the first-open visibility of the task-side action (R-01) and the handling of lowercase keys (R-02). Specifying both now would save rework in Functional Design and the Vitest cases.
