### Summary

The design is implementable and its host-API claims check out against Kandev v0.96.0. Props for the toolbar, the filter and the task indicator match, the components are exported to plugins, and the PR toolbar already follows the host layout. Two Major gaps remain: the badge detail becomes hover-only (keyboard and touch lose it, and the not-openable badge is not focusable), and the filter width/wrapper classes needed for BR4.8 are left unspecified. Neither blocks implementation; both should be settled at the gate or revised. The Minor items are consistency and test-coverage fixes.
