### Summary

The design fits the situation: the plugin owns no cloud resources, and the CI pipeline, manual install, no-secrets CI and rollback are consistent with team practices and with the NFR design. Two Major gaps remain, an incomplete traceability table that fails the sensor and a logs-only monitoring design that rests on unconfirmed Kandev behaviour with no verification step for the skeleton demo. With two Majors and no Critical the verdict is READY, and both Majors should be fixed at the gate.
