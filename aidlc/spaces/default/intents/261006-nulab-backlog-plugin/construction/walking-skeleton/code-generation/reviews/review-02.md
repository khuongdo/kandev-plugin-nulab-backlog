### Summary

The implementation is sound against the contracts: the guard is fail-closed, the 12 s plus 2 s budget holds, the secret never reaches logs or the UI, and the SDK calls match v0.96.0. Two Major gaps remain and are within the READY limit. `set_enabled` can fail after it has committed and breaks NFR1.3 (R-01), and the settings screen does not follow the switch after Save (R-02). Fix both before the skeleton checkpoint, since the manual check exercises exactly this path.
