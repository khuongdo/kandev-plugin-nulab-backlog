### Summary

The design is implementable and traces every NFR of U1, with a consistent 13-second budget and a sound secret-then-record consistency model. Two Major gaps should be fixed before code: the request-scoped redaction and `requestId` do not survive the fresh rollback context, and the epoch can regress when the secret is missing, which conflicts with contract C3. The remaining items are Minor clarifications and do not block READY (at most 2 Major).
