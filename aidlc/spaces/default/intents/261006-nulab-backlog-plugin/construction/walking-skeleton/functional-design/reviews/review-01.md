### Summary

The AC-to-rule traceability resolves and the happy path of connect is well specified. The design is not yet implementable as written. The Backlog error paths are incomplete (R-01), the secret-plus-record write has no workable rollback (R-02), the C1/C2 contracts that U1 owns are not designed (R-03), package verification does not check what AC7.1.1 requires (R-04), and the header-versus-query decision is marked as satisfied while AC1.1.7, NFR3 and components.md still say the opposite (R-05). There are five Major findings, so the verdict is NOT-READY.
