### Summary

The requirements are traceable to the Q&A and the root cause, scoped tightly, and have a good regression test (NFR3) and clear out-of-scope items. Two Major gaps remain that the human should weigh before approving: the recommended URL-install path is not specified well enough to write or verify, and nothing proves the reporter's own failing scenario is solved, because a 24 MB upload is borderline at the reproduced link speed. Both have workarounds (they can be settled in Functional Design), so the verdict is READY with these flagged.
